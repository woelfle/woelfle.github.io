## 2. General information on data processing

This website is a personal blog. It does **not** set any cookies for
tracking or analytics purposes and does **not** use any external analytics
services (such as Google Analytics, Matomo or similar). The operator does not
actively collect, process or store any personal data.

## 3. Hosting

This website is provided via **IONOS Deploy Now**. When you visit the website,
technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time of access and the
amount of data transferred are processed. No personal data is actively
collected, processed or stored.

## 4. External content

This website does **not** load any scripts, fonts or styles from external
servers (CDNs). All fonts, icons and libraries are served locally from this
website's own server. No data is therefore transferred to third-party
providers when the website is loaded.

## 5. Search function

The built-in search works entirely in the visitor's browser (client-side,
Fuse.js). No search queries are transmitted to a server.

## 6. Local storage (localStorage)

The browser's local storage (`localStorage`) is used to remember the selected
appearance (light/dark). This data never leaves the browser.

## 7. Rights of data subjects

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

- Access to your processed data (Art. 15 GDPR)
- Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
- Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
- Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- Objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

To exercise these rights, contact the responsible person named above.
