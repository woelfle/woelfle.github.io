Posts in this category explore leadership and teams: how Scrum, OKRs,
expectations, and organizational structure really shape what a group can do.
The lens is systems thinking applied to the human layer — feedback loops with
long delays, incentives that quietly steer decisions, and the constraints that
are external to the driver but internal to the system owner.
