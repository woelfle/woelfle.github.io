Posts in this category are about continuous learning: the discipline of getting
better at all of the things above. Writing a thought down as a sentence forces
you to actually have one; a system that cannot update its own beliefs is a
system that stops working. These posts think out loud about the things that are
not fully understood yet, and revise them in the open.
