Posts in this category are about the lens itself: systems thinking. Donella
Meadows defined a system as "a set of things — people, cells, molecules, or
whatever — interconnected in such a way that they produce their own pattern of
behavior over time."

The four other categories — software architecture & design, engineering
practices, leadership & teams, and continuous learning — sit downstream of this
lens. This category gathers the posts that are fundamentally about the
methodology and frameworks of systems thinking: feedback loops with long delays,
the Theory of Constraints, queuing theory, cybernetics, and Ashby's Law of
Requisite Variety — the old wisdom whose stakes rise when the input rate
changes.
