Posts tagged `platform-engineering` explore internal developer platforms — the
"buffet" model of enablement where teams serve themselves from a curated,
opinionated selection of tools rather than cooking from scratch or waiting at the
restaurant door. The platform is treated as a software product, not a side
project.
