A [Zettelkasten](https://zettelkasten.de/) is a system of slip-boxes: a network of small,
atomic notes that are connected to one another by reference rather than by category.

For this blog, the tag marks thoughts that belong to the practice of writing one claim
down as a sentence and linking it to others. It covers note-taking, how knowledge is
organized, and the discipline of turning an idea into something that can be revised and
argued with.
