## About This Blog

**Thinking in Systems** is a private software engineering blog exploring architecture, methodologies, and systems thinking.

This blog serves as a personal knowledge repository where I document insights, patterns, and lessons learned throughout my software engineering journey.

## Philosophy

I believe that the best software emerges from understanding systems holistically—not just the code, but how components interact, how teams collaborate, and how decisions compound over time.

### Core Themes

- **Systems Thinking**: Understanding software as interconnected systems rather than isolated components
- **Architecture & Design**: Patterns, principles, and trade-offs in software architecture
- **Engineering Excellence**: Practices that lead to maintainable, reliable, and scalable systems
- **Continuous Learning**: Documenting insights and evolving understanding

## About Me

I'm a software engineer passionate about building robust systems and sharing knowledge. This blog is my digital garden—a place to cultivate ideas and reflect on the craft of software engineering.
